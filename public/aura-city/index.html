Auracity Platform Architecture (Raipur)

How Auracity solves the three critical bottlenecks of Chhattisgarh's Urban Local Bodies: Land boundary discrepancies, sluggish municipal asset maintenance, and citizen opacity.

1. The Problem in Rapidly Expanding Cities

With the rapid expansion of Raipur and Atal Nagar, outdated boundary records lead to encroachment disputes along highways and lake buffers. Municipal asset records remain fragmented, and citizen grievances face delays due to lack of geotagged dispatch.

2. The Auracity Triad

  • • Bhuiyan CG Cadastre Sync: Live integration with Chhattisgarh Bhuiyan land portal & Khasra extracts to automatically audit spatial footprints.
  • • RMC IoT Telemetry Fabric: Real-time monitoring of 14,200+ municipal assets (streetlights, water pumps, waste hoppers).
  • • Citizen-AI Grievance Engine: Computer vision on citizen smartphone photos flags pothole depth, waste volume, and dark zones with automatic SLA routing.
